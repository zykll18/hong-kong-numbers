Hong Kong in Numbers — an independent guide to the city’s official statistics. Every figure on this site names its source and its year on the page where it appears.

Figures come from the Census and Statistics Department, the Rating and Valuation Department, the Government Economist’s office and the Legislative Council Research Office. Figures marked provisional are revised in later releases. This site is not affiliated with the Hong Kong SAR Government.
